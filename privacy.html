Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

What we collect

When you create an account: your name, work email, store URL, and a password (stored as a hash, never in plain text). Store web addresses you ask us to check. Images you upload for review. Payment records are handled by Gumroad; we do not store card details.

Why we use it

To run your account, perform the checks you request, prepare takedown drafts for your approval, and handle billing and support.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase: database and sign-in
  • Resend: email
  • SerpApi: reverse image search, sent only the public image address
  • Gumroad: payments
  • Vercel: hosting

How long we keep it

Account and case records are kept while your account is active, and deleted within 30 days of a deletion request or account closure, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, or to object to processing by emailing legal@alphaguardsuite.com.

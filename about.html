About AlphaGuard Security Suite

AlphaGuard Security Suite helps online sellers, creators and photographers find copies of their product images and other digital work on other websites and marketplaces.

Why we built it

Small brands often discover that a copied listing is taking their customers, and they have no quick way to find it or document it. We want to make that process faster and better documented, while keeping a person in charge of every decision.

What we do

  • Check a store's public pages for product images that appear elsewhere.
  • Record each possible match with its web address, the time it was found, and a similarity score.
  • Prepare takedown drafts for you to review. Nothing is sent without approval.

Similarity is not a legal determination. A match needs review before any action is taken.

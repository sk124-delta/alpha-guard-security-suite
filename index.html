How it works

  1. 1. Scan

    Your catalog images are checked against reverse image search once a day.

  2. 2. Detect

    Perceptual hashes compare images and flag likely matches for review.

  3. 3. Verify

    Each match is recorded with its URLs and similarity score.

  4. 4. Takedown

    Day 1: DMCA draft, sent only after approval. Day 3 and Day 7 stages are planned, not yet automated.

What we do and do not do

  • Matches are flagged for human review. Nothing is sent without an explicit confirmation from a signed-in admin.
  • Scans run on a daily schedule. They are not continuous.
  • Similarity is not a legal determination. Review all evidence before sending any notice.
